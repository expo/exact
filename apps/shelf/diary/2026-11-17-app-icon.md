### Rough
- Shelf had no icon at all, and alternates need a primary, so it gained one
  (`assets/icon.png`, any maskable) on every host, not only iOS.
- `setAppIcon(icon=name)` with `name: option<string>` does not compile: an
  `option<string>` argument to an option is `option<option<string>>`, which
  the compiler refuses. The primary is `setAppIcon()` from its own action.
- A mutation's answer is not a `resource` in a runner test; a derive over it
  reads it. The test also has to play the host's work (`take_requests`,
  `release_work`, `fulfill`) for a held send to finish.
- Under the agent with `--bind app-icon`, iOS's alert shows but a drive cannot
  press its OK, and the completion handler waits for it, so `clock data` gives
  up on the request. The XCUITest harness in `packages/quick-actions/test`
  presses it (`springboard.alerts`).
- XCUITest found two elements per row: a grouped list's cell carries the
  row's identifier as well as its button; `app.buttons[id]` picks the button.
- The disk filled twice during the runner's integration-test build, which
  needs about 3 GB.

### Lean in
- The picker is plain Contract: `role="radio"` rows with `aria-checked`, a
  29 pt picture of each icon and a checkmark, in the inset-grouped list Stats
  already is. The alert and its wording are iOS's.
- The alternate is three lines of `app.json`; the iOS build makes its set as
  it makes the primary's.

### Needed
- alternate app icons: provided by exact2 (LLP 1163.000, `alternate_icons`
  and `@exact/app-icon`)

### Checkpoints
- Under the agent: `prefer extension app-icon.icons=["Paper"]`, the App Icon
  section appears; tapping Paper holds an `app-icon` ticket, and `tap @N ok`
  checks Paper.
- On the iPhone 18 Pro simulator (iOS 27.2): Paper brings iOS's alert, and
  after OK the Home Screen shows Paper; a relaunch keeps it; choosing it again
  shows no alert; Shelf's own icon comes back with the alert.
